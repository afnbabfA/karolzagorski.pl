About

I am a medical doctor and resident physician in anatomic pathology in Wrocław, Poland. My clinical and academic focus centers on surgical oncopathology, digitized whole-slide imaging (WSI), and translational computational oncology.

My current research investigates the integration of quantum-classical hybrid machine learning algorithms and deep feature extractors for survival prediction and prognostic stratification in rare malignancies, with a particular focus on uveal melanoma.

Research & Clinical Interests

  • Computational Pathology & WSI: Deep learning pipelines for automated histological feature extraction, tumor microenvironment profiling, and risk stratification.
  • Quantum Machine Learning (QML): Variational quantum circuits (VQC) and hybrid neural architectures applied to high-dimensional biomedical data.
  • Ophthalmic & Neural Pathology: Biomarkers, genomic alterations, and histoprognostic factors in intraocular melanomas and ocular adnexal tumors.
  • Diagnostic Histopathology: Routine and specialized diagnostic biopsy assessment, immunohistochemistry, and molecular pathology.
